Sebastian Elgueta Godoy.

PROFILE

Public policy, regulation and regulated markets.

Sebastian Elgueta Godoy is a Chilean sociologist and public affairs consultant with over 13 years of experience across public institutions, legislative advisory work, consulting and applied research.

His work explores regulated markets and public policies across sectors including healthcare, energy, tourism and technology, with particular attention to institutional decision-making, regulatory frameworks and territorial contexts in Chile and Latin America.

He combines institutional analysis with public data, longitudinal indicators, comparative research and territorial evidence to understand complex public and sectoral challenges.

AREAS OF WORK

Public affairs

Political, regulatory and institutional analysis, stakeholder environments and strategic positioning.

Regulated markets

Healthcare, energy, tourism and technology, examined through public policy, regulatory and institutional perspectives.

Applied research

Open data, longitudinal series, comparative research, digital inclusion and territorial analysis.

PUBLIC WORK

Public projects include Latin America Digital Infrastructure, Chile Digital Inclusion, the Atlas de la Desconexión Digital de Chile, longitudinal Internet performance research, Chile State Institutional Map and Stakeholder Routes Chile.
