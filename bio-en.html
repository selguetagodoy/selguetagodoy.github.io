Sebastian Elgueta Godoy.

Sociologist and public affairs professional working at the intersection of public policy, telecommunications, digital infrastructure, data centers, connectivity and territorial development.

PROFILE

Policy, infrastructure and territory.

Sebastian Elgueta Godoy is a Chilean sociologist and public affairs professional with more than 13 years of experience across the public sector, legislative advisory work, strategic consulting and applied research.

His work focuses on public policy, regulation, telecommunications, digital infrastructure, data centers, connectivity and territorial development, with particular attention to how institutional, regulatory, economic and infrastructure conditions shape investment and digital transformation in Chile and Latin America.

He combines institutional analysis with public data, longitudinal indicators, international benchmarking and territorial analysis to support decision-making in complex regulatory and infrastructure environments.

AREAS OF WORK

Public affairs

Political, regulatory and institutional analysis, stakeholder environments and strategic positioning.

Digital infrastructure

Data centers, telecommunications, connectivity, cloud and the enabling conditions for digital investment.

Applied research

Open data, longitudinal series, comparative research, digital inclusion and territorial analysis.

PUBLIC WORK

Public projects include Latin America Digital Infrastructure, Chile Digital Inclusion, the Atlas de la Desconexión Digital de Chile and longitudinal Internet performance research.
