# Research by Sebastián Elgueta Godoy

## Latin America Digital Infrastructure

A source-backed comparative project on data centers, cloud regions, Internet exchange points, submarine connectivity, energy and institutional conditions in Latin America.

Observatory: https://selguetagodoy.github.io/latin-america-digital-infrastructure/

Dataset page: https://selguetagodoy.github.io/dataset-latin-america-digital-infrastructure.html

Repository: https://github.com/selguetagodoy/latin-america-digital-infrastructure

## Chile Digital Inclusion

A public data and research project on digital inclusion, connectivity, broadband, infrastructure and territorial gaps in Chile.

Dataset page: https://selguetagodoy.github.io/dataset-chile-digital-inclusion.html

Repository: https://github.com/selguetagodoy/Chile-Digital-Inclusion

## Atlas de la Desconexión Digital de Chile

A territorial research project that distinguishes the intensity of digital connectivity gaps from their social scale across Chilean municipalities.

Repository: https://github.com/selguetagodoy/atlas-desconexion-digital-chile.

External reference: https://cotel.cl/cotel-presenta-el-atlas-de-la-desconexion-digital/

## Internet performance research

Longitudinal analysis of fixed and mobile Internet performance using publicly available evidence while preserving methodological breaks and missing values.

Repository: https://github.com/selguetagodoy/Ookla

## Methodological principles

- Preserve missing values when evidence is unavailable.
- Do not invent or silently interpolate observations.
- Keep different statistical universes and methodologies explicit.
- Prefer public and primary sources.
- Separate operational infrastructure from planned or announced capacity.
- Document source provenance and comparability limitations.
