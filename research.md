## Methodological principles

- Preserve missing values when evidence is unavailable.
- Do not invent, interpolate or silently overwrite observations.
- Keep statistical universes, source families and methodological breaks explicit.
- Prefer primary, official and technically authoritative sources.
- Separate operational infrastructure from planned or announced capacity.
- Distinguish observed values, proxies and derived indicators.
- Document source provenance, transformation logic and comparability limits.
- Use versioned releases and persistent identifiers for citable public research.
