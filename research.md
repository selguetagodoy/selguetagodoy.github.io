## Internet performance research

Longitudinal analysis of fixed and mobile Internet performance using publicly available evidence while preserving methodological breaks and missing values.

Repository: https://github.com/selguetagodoy/Ookla

## Methodological principles

- Preserve missing values when evidence is unavailable.
- Do not invent or silently interpolate observations.
- Keep different statistical universes and methodologies explicit.
- Prefer public and primary sources.
- Separate operational infrastructure from planned or announced capacity.
- Document source provenance and comparability limitations.
